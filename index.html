Overview
~6 hours of expert duo free improvisations with per-player audio stems and synchronized video. Here we show one clip from each of the 6 musician pairings.
Annotations
Bidirectional: each performer annotated their own intentions and their perception of their partner's actions using our proposed communication model.
How to use
Click anywhere on an annotation timeline to seek the video to that moment. Bars show improvisation states derived from each player's annotation perspective.
